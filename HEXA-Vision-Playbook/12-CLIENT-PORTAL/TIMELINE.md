# ⌛ VISUAL TIMELINE & MILESTONE SPECIFICATIONS

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Scope:** Interactive Timeline | **Standard:** Interactive Gantt/Milestone View

---

## 1. OVERVIEW & FEATURES

The Interactive Timeline module displays completed, in-progress, and upcoming milestones for architectural visualization projects (*Schematic Design $\rightarrow$ 3D Modeling $\rightarrow$ Material/Lighting Review $\rightarrow$ Final Rendering $\rightarrow$ Handover*).

---

## 2. INTERACTIVE MILESTONE APPROVAL

Clients can click on specific milestone nodes to:
- Preview draft 3D renders or images.
- Leave contextual feedback annotations.
- Click **"Approve Phase"** to advance project status in Odoo.
