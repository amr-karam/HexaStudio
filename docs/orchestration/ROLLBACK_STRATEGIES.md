# HEXA STUDIO — ROLLBACK STRATEGIES

## 1. PHASE 1: SPECIFICATION

### Conditions for Rollback
- Incomplete or inaccurate system boundaries
- Incomplete or inaccurate technical stack
- Incomplete or inaccurate key features
- Incomplete or inaccurate non-functional requirements
- Incomplete or inaccurate integration points
- Incomplete or inaccurate CMS requirements
- Incomplete or inaccurate infrastructure expectations
- Incomplete or inaccurate architecture principles
- Incomplete or inaccurate data flow
- Incomplete or inaccurate security architecture
- Incomplete or inaccurate performance targets
- Incomplete or inaccurate documentation requirements
- Incomplete or inaccurate quality gates

### Steps for Rollback
- Revert to previous specification documents
- Re-evaluate system boundaries, technical stack, key features, non-functional requirements, integration points, CMS requirements, infrastructure expectations, architecture principles, data flow, security architecture, performance targets, documentation requirements, and quality gates

## 2. PHASE 2: PLANNING

### Conditions for Rollback
- Incomplete or inaccurate work decomposition
- Incomplete or inaccurate milestones and deliverables
- Incomplete or inaccurate dependencies and critical paths
- Incomplete or inaccurate Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)
- Incomplete or inaccurate risks and mitigation strategies
- Incomplete or inaccurate detailed execution plan

### Steps for Rollback
- Revert to previous planning documents
- Re-evaluate work decomposition, milestones and deliverables, dependencies and critical paths, Architecture Decision Records (ADRs), risks and mitigation strategies, and detailed execution plan

## 3. PHASE 3: ORCHESTRATION

### Conditions for Rollback
- Incomplete or inaccurate execution sequence
- Incomplete or inaccurate coordination of subsystems and components
- Incomplete or inaccurate safe and incremental changes
- Incomplete or inaccurate prevention of destructive or irreversible operations
- Incomplete or inaccurate validation of each step before proceeding

### Steps for Rollback
- Revert to previous orchestration documents
- Re-evaluate execution sequence, coordination of subsystems and components, safe and incremental changes, prevention of destructive or irreversible operations, and validation of each step before proceeding

## 4. PHASE 4: IMPLEMENTATION

### Conditions for Rollback
- Incomplete or inaccurate frontend components
- Incomplete or inaccurate backend services
- Incomplete or inaccurate CMS integration
- Incomplete or inaccurate infrastructure setup
- Incomplete or inaccurate AI features
- Incomplete or inaccurate client portal
- Incomplete or inaccurate ERP integration

### Steps for Rollback
- Revert to previous implementation documents
- Re-evaluate frontend components, backend services, CMS integration, infrastructure setup, AI features, client portal, and ERP integration

## 5. PHASE 5: REVIEW

### Conditions for Rollback
- Incomplete or inaccurate deliverables against requirements
- Incomplete or inaccurate quality assurance gates
- Incomplete or inaccurate system integrity and stability
- Incomplete or inaccurate comprehensive final report
- Incomplete or inaccurate system readiness status

### Steps for Rollback
- Revert to previous review documents
- Re-evaluate all deliverables against requirements, quality assurance gates, system integrity and stability, comprehensive final report, and system readiness status

## 6. FINAL READINESS VERDICT

The system is ready for production if all quality gates are passed and all requirements are met.
