# 📊 PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS & FORECASTING MODEL SPECIFICATION

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Scope:** Predictive Analytics Engine | **Standard:** ML Time-Series & Demand Forecasting

---

## 1. OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVES

The Forecasting Engine analyzes historical project timelines, lead velocity, resource allocation, and market demand to forecast project delivery dates, cash flow, and team utilization.

---

## 2. KEY FORECASTING MODELS

1. **Timeline Forecasting**: Predicts project completion dates based on historical Odoo milestone variance.
2. **Revenue & Cash Flow Forecasting**: Projects monthly billing schedules from active client portal contracts.
3. **Resource Capacity Forecasting**: Predicts team utilization bottlenecks 30–90 days in advance.
