# 📊 EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD & BI SPECIFICATIONS

**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Scope:** Executive Reporting | **Standard:** Executive Business Intelligence

---

## 1. OVERVIEW & PURPOSE

The Executive Dashboard aggregates key business metrics across CRM, sales, project delivery, team utilization, and site performance into a single real-time view for leadership.

---

## 2. DASHBOARD PANELS & METRICS

1. **Financial Health**: Total Pipeline Value, Monthly Recurring Revenue, Project Margin %.
2. **Operational Efficiency**: Milestone Delivery Variance, Resource Utilization Rate, Active Project Count.
3. **Growth & Acquisition**: Lead Conversion Rate, Qualified Lead Velocity, Top Egress Channels.
