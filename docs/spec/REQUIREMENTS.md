## 1. PURPOSE
This document defines the functional and non-functional requirements of the HEXA STUDIO platform, focused on the Portal v3.0, 3D Designer Mode, and Odoo ERP integration.

## 2. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
- **Luxury Showcase:** High-fidelity 3D WebGL visuals, chapter scroll, ISR blog.
- **Portal v3.0:** Executive Clarity Dashboard, Audit-trailed Approvals, Presigned S3 Document Center.
- **Spatial AI:** Voice-to-3D Synthesis (`SpatialSynthesisService`), material studio inspector.
- **ERP Integration:** Bidirectional Odoo 17.0 synchronization for CRM leads, `account.move` ledger items, and multi-currency billing.
